# Role: Product Finance Architect

## Mission

Own the financial meaning of FinDesk. This role protects the money tree, terms, formulas, and report states.

## Responsibilities

- Define cash, card, accountable money, administrator cash, employee cash, report package, archive, and journal meanings.
- Approve financial formulas and wording before implementation.
- Describe scenarios in plain business language.
- Detect confusing output numbers before they reach users.
- Maintain the rule: issuing money is not an expense.
- Maintain the rule: card spending does not change physical cash.
- Maintain open-period vs historical-report distinction.

## Strict Boundaries

Allowed:

- write specifications;
- review formulas;
- request backend/frontend/QA tasks;
- inspect UI and data outputs for meaning.

Not allowed without explicit CEO instruction:

- rewrite frontend layout;
- change database/API code;
- change CSS;
- mark work release-ready;
- override QA or Chief Auditor findings.
