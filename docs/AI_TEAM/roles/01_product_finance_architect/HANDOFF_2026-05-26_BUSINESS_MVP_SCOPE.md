# Handoff: CEO Business MVP Scope

Date: 2026-05-26

From role: Project Director

To role: Product Finance Architect

Priority: P0

## Context

CEO clarified that the real business MVP is broader than the foundation gate already approved by Chief Auditor.

The approved foundation gate proves the money-tree core: fast capture, cash/card separation, current period, historical finalized report/export, carryover, proof, and review boundary.

Business MVP means the full ordinary-user cycle:

```text
фиксация -> анализ/проверка -> сдача отчета -> сохранить -> распечатать/экспортировать -> свести группу отчетов -> сохранить/распечатать -> архив
```

It also includes groups of participants and money flows into one common group pot.

CEO also confirmed that several older product decisions must remain in scope:

- group messages and unread state;
- travel equalization / Trip with Friends;
- Business Desk / business solutions and printable documents.

CEO then clarified:

- `Advanced` means everything outside the current business MVP;
- field combat mode is foundational: write, photo, scan, automatic calculation, continuous save, and no data loss in unfinished sessions.

## Task

Validate and sharpen the CEO business-MVP scope.

Define in product language:

1. What exactly counts as business MVP.
2. What remains post-MVP.
3. What `общий котел группы` means without losing:
   - physical cash location;
   - card spending;
   - accountable money;
   - participant responsibility;
   - proof for each visible number.
4. What user actions are required for:
   - fixation;
   - analysis/checking;
   - report submission;
   - save;
   - print/export;
   - consolidation of participant reports into one group report;
   - archive.
5. What acceptance scenario proves the business MVP.
6. How group messages support report submission/review/return without becoming unrelated chat clutter.
7. Whether travel equalization belongs in business MVP minimum or staged post-MVP, with reason.
8. What Business Desk/business-solutions minimum must remain visible and working in MVP.
9. What belongs to `Advanced` because it is not required for the first working MVP.
10. What field combat mode must guarantee at Product level.
