Executive Summary

FinDesk now has a virtual specialist office for disciplined product preparation. The goal is to stop mixing financial architecture, backend data logic, frontend UX, QA, and audit into one overloaded chat.

The office has five specialist roles: Product Finance Architect, Backend Data Engineer, Frontend UX Engineer, QA Release Engineer, and Chief Auditor.

Why The Office Exists

The product is financially sensitive. The main risk is not only a wrong number on one screen. The real risk is losing the money tree: who holds physical cash, what was paid by card, what is accountable money, what was spent, what remains, what is checked, what is included in the final report, and what is only archived or recorded in the journal.

Operating Model

  1. Every specialist chat runs baseline checks.
  2. Every specialist chat reads office documents and its own role cabinet.
  3. Every specialist chat stays inside role boundaries.
  4. Findings are written in each cabinet.
  5. Cross-role tasks are written in TASKS_TO_OTHERS.md.
  6. Major decisions are written in 05_DECISIONS.md.
  7. Release readiness is controlled by Chief Auditor in RELEASE_GATE.md.

CEO Control Points

  1. Does every shown number answer whose money it is?
  2. Is physical cash separated from card spending?
  3. Are employee remainders carried forward correctly?
  4. Does the administrator also appear in live reports and summaries?
  5. Can old reports be opened without polluting the open period?
  6. Is the phone workflow compact enough for real field use?
  7. Does Excel/Google export look like a business report, not a technical dump?
  8. Did QA verify desktop, tablet, and mobile?
  9. Did Chief Auditor clear P0/P1 risks?

Recommendation

Use this office immediately for the next phase: Product Finance Architect finalizes terminology, Backend Data Engineer verifies snapshots and carryover, Frontend UX Engineer cleans page distribution, QA Release Engineer tests core scenarios, and Chief Auditor reviews contradictions.
