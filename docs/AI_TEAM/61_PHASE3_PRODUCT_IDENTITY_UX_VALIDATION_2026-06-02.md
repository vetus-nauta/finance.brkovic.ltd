# FinDesk Phase 3 - Product Identity, UX Validation And Cohesion

Date: 2026-06-02
Source: `https://drive.google.com/file/d/1S11kHRLFfb5yidEDNpGO5hsBd6-UtlZa/view?usp=drive_link`

## Purpose

Phase 3 ensures that Phase 1 logic, Phase 2 architecture and final UX become one coherent product.

## Core Rule

Do not invent a new FinDesk.

Do not redesign business logic.

Express the approved product.

## Product Identity

FinDesk is:

- a money journal;
- a working tool;
- a habit;
- an operational workspace.

FinDesk is not:

- accounting software;
- ERP;
- CRM;
- banking application;
- investment application;
- analytics dashboard;
- landing page.

## Brand Thought

Деньги исчезают тихо.

Потратил - запиши.

Получил - запиши.

## Style Direction

Operational Luxury Minimalism.

Avoid:

- card-in-card-in-card;
- dashboard overload;
- notebook metaphors;
- paper textures;
- vintage effects;
- WordPress feeling;
- SaaS template feeling.

## Validation Rules

- Live Journal remains a clean operational screen.
- Team Workspace remains a People Screen.
- Employee Card remains simple.
- Cash and Card remain separate.
- Reports contain Cash, Card and Total sections.
- Old FinDesk interface remnants must disappear.

## Physical QA

Required device surfaces:

- Desktop;
- iPhone;
- Android;
- iPad.

User must understand without explanation:

- what FinDesk is;
- how to start;
- where to record money;
- where to see people;
- where to submit journals;
- where to build reports.

## Success Definition

People use FinDesk naturally.

People stop trying to remember money movements.

FinDesk becomes part of daily life.

## Director Interpretation

Phase 3 is not permission to create a new interface idea from scratch.

Phase 3 starts after the Phase 2 local implementation passes authenticated QA. Its job is to expose the already-approved FinDesk as one coherent product with a higher UX bar.

Current order:

1. Finish Phase 2 authenticated local QA.
2. Confirm first-class workflow behavior.
3. Then run Phase 3 product identity and physical UX validation.
